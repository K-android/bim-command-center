========================================================================
BIM COMMAND CENTER — CENTRAL FAMILIES & BLOCKS LIBRARY
========================================================================

Place your company-standard Revit Families (.rfa) and CAD/Rhino Blocks (.dwg, .3dm)
in this folder or any of the category subfolders:

- Architectural/       (Doors, Windows, Furniture, Casework, Fixtures)
- Structural/          (Columns, Beams, Framing, Foundations, Trusses)
- MEP/                 (Mechanical, Electrical, Lighting, Plumbing, Equipment)
- Annotation & Details/(2D Detail Items, Title Blocks, Symbols, Tags)
- Blocks/              (Standard CAD DWG details, 3D Rhino blocks, SketchUp)

Any user on the team can browse, preview 3D thumbnails, and load or place
these components with 1 click directly inside Revit via the BIM Command Center
ribbon button: 'Families & Blocks'.
========================================================================
